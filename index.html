What to check before you order

SpanBuy shoe finds and clothing size checks

For low-top sneakers, compare insole length in centimeters, toe-box width, and outsole photos. Use a paired US, UK, and EU shoe size chart as a starting point. Compare the seller’s measurements with a pair you already own.

For an oversized hoodie or graphic T-shirt, measure chest width flat and check body length and shoulder width. For cargo trousers, compare waist and inseam. For a football jersey, check sleeve length and print placement.

SpanBuy QC photos and product links

Open the original seller listing and record the item URL, selected color and size, and date checked. For a warehouse QC photo, confirm it shows your selected variant. Compare stitching, label placement, and material texture across several angles.

For a tote, check strap drop and interior pockets. For a belt, ask where its length is measured from. Note a fitted cap’s circumference, a silk scarf’s flat dimensions, or a necklace’s clasp type and chain length.

SpanBuy shipping cost checklist

For a delivered-cost estimate, record the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, and international postage in one currency. Ask which parcel dimensions count toward volumetric weight before combining bulky shoes and jackets.

For the US, UK, or EU, confirm the carrier’s destination rules and whether its quote includes duties and taxes. Save the quote date, packed weight, and any return deadline alongside the product link.

Frequently asked questions

What is a SpanBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist organized by category. A useful row records the original product link, exact variant, measurements or QC notes, and when you last checked the listing.

Where do the product categories lead?

Each category opens the corresponding CAFANS catalog page. Check the destination listing for current availability, size options, and seller terms.

Can QC photos verify authenticity?

No. QC photos can show visible construction, condition, and variant details. Use documented provenance or a qualified authentication service when authenticity matters.

How should I compare shipping quotes?

Compare quotes for the same destination, packed dimensions, and weight. Check whether each quote includes handling fees and whether duties or taxes are collected separately.
